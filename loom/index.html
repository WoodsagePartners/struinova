Loom · a tool from Struinova

Agree on the Why and the What.
Then go looking for the How.

Most teams reach for an innovation — automation, AI, a new tool — before they’ve agreed on what’s actually happening and why it hurts. Loom is where you map the real process first, with the tools to actually investigate it, so whatever comes next has something real to aim at.

The order matters

The How is the easy part to get excited about — it’s also the part that fails silently when it’s aimed at the wrong step. An AI agent bolted onto a process nobody’s mapped just moves the bottleneck; it doesn’t remove it. Loom holds the line on sequence: lay out the process as it actually runs today — who touches it, where it waits, where it breaks — before anyone picks a technique to fix it.

The investigation

See it every way that matters

Still the What and the Why — just under a few different lights. Loom doesn’t skip ahead to a fix; it hands you the tools to actually understand what’s happening first.

01 // SIMULATE

Process Simulator

Run the process forward and watch where time actually pools — the real bottleneck, not the one everyone blames.

02 // SEE THE FRICTION

Rework & bottleneck view

Every loop-back, every stall, every handoff that waits days — named and shown on the map, not buried in someone’s complaint.

03 // AGENT READINESS

Agent readiness

Step by step, see where an AI or an automation would actually fit today’s process — before anyone commits to building it.

04 // PRICE IT

Operating cost

Attach real volume, exception rate, and a blended labor rate, and the bottleneck gets a dollar sign, not just a shape.

05 // ASK IT

Ask Tally

Ask a plain question — typed or spoken — about what’s on the canvas, and get an answer that points straight at the steps.
